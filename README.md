##  Executive Summary – Sales Analysis Dashboard

The Sales Analysis dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance across orders, revenue, customer behavior, and product trends.

###  Overall Performance

The business has generated a **total revenue of ₹1,51,619** from **39 orders**, indicating a moderate transaction volume with a **healthy average customer spending of ₹3,887.67**. The **average order-to-delivery time is ~5.18 days**, reflecting an efficient delivery process.

---

###  Revenue Insights

* **Occasion-wise:**
  Revenue is highest for **Anniversary and Birthday occasions**, indicating strong demand for celebration-based purchases.

* **Category-wise:**
  **Colors and Sweets** are the top-performing categories, contributing the highest share of revenue, while categories like **Mugs and Raksha Bandhan items** show lower performance.

---

###  Time-Based Trends

* **Monthly Trend:**
  Revenue shows a **significant upward trend**, with a sharp increase in later months, suggesting seasonal demand or successful campaigns.

* **Hourly Trend:**
  Peak order activity occurs during **evening hours (around 6 PM)**, indicating prime customer engagement time.

---

###  Product & Location Insights

* **Top Products:**
  Products like **Deserunt Box and Nihil Box** generate the highest revenue, making them key contributors to sales.

* **Top Cities:**
  Cities such as **Imphal, Kavali, and Dhanbad** (based on visible data) show higher order volumes, indicating strong regional demand.

---

###  Customer Behavior

* Customers demonstrate **high spending per order**, suggesting a preference for premium or bundled products.
* Occasion-based purchasing behavior is prominent, especially for **Anniversaries and Birthdays**.
